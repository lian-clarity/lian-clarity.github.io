Chengzhen Protocol

A non-violent cognitive instrument. It does not attack or explain the target — it lets the hidden self-referential folds reveal themselves.

"The probe does not manufacture truth. It lets what is already there unfold."

Step 01
Anchor Discrimination
Name the thing. Then un-name it. In the gap between "this is" and "this is not," the first semantic fissure appears.
Step 02
Name the Loop
Watch how the system cycles between A and not-A. Identify its unconscious self-referential habit. Name it — without adjectives.
Step 03
Mirror from Afar
Borrow a structure from an unrelated system. Use it to re-describe the loop. Watch for interference — the sign of something third emerging.
Step 04
Withdraw & Return
Pull back every disturbance. "I withdraw my discrimination." Fold the probe into a single sentence. Archive it. Silence resumes.
