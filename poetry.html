Poem Eye · 诗眼
Light Band · 光线带
A 3-5mm LED strip woven into the waistline, breathing in rhythm with human heartbeat (60-80 bpm). Slow breath while cruising, faster when accelerating. At rest, a faint pulse — like a sleeping creature. By night, in a parking lot full of cold metal, only the Poetry Wind is alive.
Design Philosophy
Eastern whitespace aesthetics meet modern engineering. The body is a clean, flowing sentence — rhythmic but not loud. Like well-written running script (行书). Every surface curvature is G2 continuous: light transitions must be slow and gentle, never sharp.
Interior Principle
Subtraction: 80% of physical buttons removed, 5-7 tactile knobs retained. No screen on the dashboard — core information via HUD projection. Warm tones, touch-first materials. The screen-free cockpit frees the driver's gaze and mind.

Poem Eye · 诗眼
Celestial Canopy · 天幕
Full-roof electrochromic glass that shifts from transparent to semi-opaque starfield. The gradient is not uniform — center stays clearest (like looking up from a mountaintop), edges grow hazy (like cloud sea). Covers all three rows. A child in the third row can see the stars. The AI pairs it with ambient scenes: "Spring Rain" makes it misty, "Solitude" slowly dims it like closing eyes.
Design Philosophy
A mobile mountain — steady, protective, grounded. Bamboo Green (竹青) paint blends into forest and valley. Surface tension is slightly more pronounced than Feng, like a mountain's silhouette: curvature varies but transitions remain smooth. The proportion pursues stability: shoulder-width to height ≈ 1.2:1.
Safety is the Greatest Poetry
Blade battery standard across all trims. L2+ active safety standard. No safety discounts between high and low configurations. A family vehicle's first poem is protection — this is the design principle that overrides all others.

Poem Eye · 诗眼
Ink-wash Grille · 水墨格栅
Parametric ink-wash texture on the active grille — each angle reveals different light and shadow, like a living painting. The ink flows from center outward, "dispersing" like a drop in water. 5-7 standardized ink-stamp units combine infinitely, making every car unique. As the active grille opens and closes for cooling, the texture shifts — revealing that beauty has function. This is "the visualization of energy flow."
Design Philosophy
Dai-color (黛色) — near-ink with deep purple undertones. A gentleman in proper attire: composed, unfathomable. Proportion pursues dignity: length-to-height ≈ 1.8:1, elongated but not low. The most refined surfaces in the series — subtle curvature changes create rich, water-like reflections. A 300-year design lifespan: no trendy tech elements, no glowing logos.
Executive Cabin
Active noise cancellation creates profound silence. Independent executive seats with maximum recline. Air suspension adapts to road conditions. The cabin is a mobile study and meditation chamber — "a room of one's own" that moves. Ceramic-coated paint feels warm as jade, not cold as metal.
